## PMIC investigation

TI's TPS65217C sequencing guide specifically addresses AM335x ZCZ. Default rails are DCDC1=1.5 V for DDR, DCDC2=1.1 V for MPU, DCDC3=1.1 V for core, LDO1=1.8 V for VDDS/RTC, LDO3/LS1=1.8 V for PLL/analog/SRAM and LDO4/LS2=3.3 V for appropriate high-voltage I/O/USB analog domains. The default MPU voltage does not qualify 1 GHz operation; firmware must select a supported voltage/OPP. Every CPU domain and decoupling requirement needs its own verified connection.

PMIC PGOOD drives TPS3808 MR; its3.3V SENSE monitors the additional MMC0/card rail before releasing CPU PWRONRSTn at1.8V. PGOOD is push-pull and must not be wired together with the supervisor's RESET output. LDO_PGOOD drives RTC_PWRONRSTn and PMIC_PWR_EN controls PWR_EN. PMIC nRESET is a shutdown input. INT_LDO/BYPASS cannot power external loads. The C variant lacks RTC-only mode. Its charger is limited to700mA and bucks to1.2A each; the full power/thermal budget remains incomplete.

The source draft wires 73 CPU supply terminals, 43 CPU grounds, every RAM power/ground/reference/calibration terminal, PMIC buck stages and reset/enable/I2C. It includes 98 CPU/RAM decouplers, including 20 CPU and 12 RAM 0402 DDR capacitors on the bottom side and two bulk capacitors per package. An actual generated-netlist audit checks these connections, nominal counts, internal-output isolation and nearby terminal distances. The RAM supply/ground and 14 bypass capacitor connections now have independently checked copper; the rest of the powered host is incomplete. VPP is explicitly NC for functional GP operation, following TI's AM3352-specific guidance.

HV1–3 use1.8V; HV4 (MMC0) shares PERIPH_3V3 with the card, while HV5/6 and USB analog3.3V stay on IO_3V3. Conservative CPU-only totals are55mA on LDO1,265mA on LDO3,230mA on LDO4 and50mA on the separate peripheral rail. Memory/card/display/audio transients and losses still need a full budget.
